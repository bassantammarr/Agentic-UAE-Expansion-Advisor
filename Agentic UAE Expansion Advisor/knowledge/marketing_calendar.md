# Marketing Calendar and Channels

## Ramadan
- Demand shifts to iftar (sunset) and suhoor (late night); daytime dine-in drops sharply.
- Run iftar family boxes and suhoor late-night deals; extend operating hours at night.
- Emphasise generosity, family sharing and community values in creative.
- Ramadan dates move about 11 days earlier each year in the Gregorian calendar; plan campaigns around the lunar calendar.

## Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha
- Peak periods for family gatherings and group orders.
- Offer large sharing platters and family bundles; run gifting or voucher promotions.

## UAE National Day (2 December)
- Strong patriotic engagement; brands run red, green, white and black themed campaigns.
- Limited-edition items and themed packaging work well; keep messaging respectful of national symbols.

## Other moments
- Dubai Shopping Festival (winter) and Dubai Summer Surprises: mall footfall and tourist traffic.
- Back-to-school season: family meal deals.
- Major sporting events and concerts on Yas Island: event-night promotions.
- Cooler months (roughly October–April) favour outdoor seating; summer favours delivery.

## Channels
- Instagram and TikTok: food content, behind-the-scenes, cheese pull and grilling videos.
- Local food influencers in Dubai and Abu Dhabi for launch tastings.
- Talabat in-app promotions and featured placements for launch visibility.
- Google Maps listing with photos and fast review responses.

## Brand positioning
- Lead with Jordanian authenticity: family recipes, Jordanian spices, generous meat portions.
- Bilingual Arabic and English content.
- Tell the founder's story; homesick Jordanian and Levantine expats are a natural early community.
