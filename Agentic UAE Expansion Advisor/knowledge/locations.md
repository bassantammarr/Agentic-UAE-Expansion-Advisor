# UAE Location Notes

Internal reference notes. Rents and footfall are indicative and should be verified with a broker before signing.

## Dubai Marina and JBR
- Very high footfall from residents, tourists and weekend visitors; strong evening and late-night demand.
- Rents among the highest in Dubai; promenade units carry a premium over inner-street units.
- Dense apartment towers make it one of the strongest delivery catchments in the city.
- Tradeoff: brand visibility and tourist trade vs. high rent and intense F&B competition.
- Fit for meaty pizza and kebab: strong for late-night and group orders; consider a smaller dine-in footprint with a large delivery kitchen.

## Downtown Dubai and Business Bay
- Downtown: premium, tourist-heavy, mall-driven (Dubai Mall area); high rent and high average spend.
- Business Bay: office workers and young professionals; strong weekday lunch and evening delivery.
- Tradeoff: Business Bay offers lower rent than Downtown with strong delivery density, but less weekend tourist traffic.
- Fit: Business Bay is a good candidate for a first flagship plus delivery hub; office lunch combos (35–45 AED) perform well.

## DIFC
- High-income professionals, premium price tolerance, busy weekday lunch, quieter weekends.
- Best for a premium fast-casual format; less suited to family-sized value orders.

## Jumeirah and Al Barsha
- Jumeirah: affluent families and villas; good for family bundles and weekend dine-in.
- Al Barsha: mixed-income residents, large Arab expat community, close to Mall of the Emirates; more moderate rents.
- Fit: Al Barsha is a strong value location for an authentic Jordanian concept; Levantine flavours resonate with the Arab expat base.

## Abu Dhabi: Yas Island and Saadiyat Island
- Yas Island: leisure destination (theme parks, Yas Mall, events); peaks on weekends and during events.
- Saadiyat Island: affluent residents and cultural district; premium positioning, lower density.
- Tradeoff: event-driven spikes vs. quieter weekdays; seasonal tourism matters.

## Abu Dhabi: Al Reem, Khalidiyah and Al Maryah
- Al Reem Island: dense residential towers, young families and professionals; strong delivery demand.
- Khalidiyah: established city neighbourhood with Arab families; good for authentic, family-oriented concepts.
- Al Maryah Island: financial district (ADGM), weekday lunch traffic, premium malls.
- Fit: Al Reem is a strong first Abu Dhabi delivery hub; Khalidiyah suits dine-in family positioning.

## Recommended rollout logic
- Start with one Dubai location that combines delivery density and moderate rent (Business Bay or Al Barsha).
- Use a cloud kitchen to test additional catchments before committing to dine-in leases.
- Enter Abu Dhabi second, starting with Al Reem Island for delivery.
