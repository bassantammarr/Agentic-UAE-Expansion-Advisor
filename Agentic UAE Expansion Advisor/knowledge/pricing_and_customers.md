# Pricing and Customer Segments

Prices are indicative ranges for casual dining in Dubai and Abu Dhabi and include 5% VAT.

## Customer segments

Young Professionals
Characteristics:
- Live in towers in Business Bay, Marina, JLT, Al Reem
- Order delivery frequently on weekdays and late at night
Spending Behavior:
- 35–55 AED per individual meal

Arab Expat Families
Characteristics:
- Strong affinity for Levantine flavours and Jordanian authenticity
- Concentrated in Al Barsha, Khalidiyah, Sharjah-border areas
Spending Behavior:
- 120–200 AED per family order

Emirati Families
Characteristics:
- Value quality, generous portions and premium presentation
- Large group orders, especially weekends and Ramadan
Spending Behavior:
- 150–250 AED per family order

Tourists and Visitors
Characteristics:
- Concentrated in Marina, JBR, Downtown, Yas Island
- Seek authentic regional food and quick service
Spending Behavior:
- 45–70 AED per person

## Price positioning
- Position in the mid-market: above mass shawarma outlets, below premium dine-in Lebanese restaurants.
- Kebab sandwich: 25–38 AED; sandwich combo: 38–48 AED.
- Meaty pizza (medium): 45–65 AED; large: 60–85 AED.
- Family bundle (2 large pizzas + sandwiches + sides): 140–190 AED.

## Pricing tactics
- Use charm pricing (e.g. 39 AED, 59 AED) on combos.
- Offer office lunch deals in business districts on weekdays.
- Keep a hero value item to win on delivery app rankings.
- Review food cost monthly; target food cost of roughly 28–33% of menu price.

## Competitive reference
- Local and regional shawarma chains compete on low price and speed.
- International pizza chains compete on promotions and delivery speed.
- Differentiation: meat-heavy Jordanian recipes and authenticity, not lowest price.
