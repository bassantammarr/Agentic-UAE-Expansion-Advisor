# Delivery Platforms

## Talabat
- The leading food delivery platform in the UAE by order volume; the priority channel for launch.
- Strong ranking factors: ratings, delivery time, menu photos, promotions and order acceptance rate.
- Commission is a significant cost (often in the range of 20–30% depending on terms and whether the platform's riders are used); negotiate launch terms and price delivery menus accordingly.
- Talabat Pro subscribers expect free delivery and deals; joining platform promotions boosts visibility.

## Deliveroo and Careem
- Deliveroo: strong among higher-income customers in Dubai; useful secondary channel.
- Careem Food: integrated with the Careem super-app and its subscription; useful for reach.
- Recommendation: launch on Talabat first, add Deliveroo and Careem once operations are stable.

## Delivery menu design
- Delivery menu prices are commonly set 10–20% above dine-in to offset commission.
- Pizza travels well; kebab sandwiches need packaging that keeps bread from going soggy (vented wraps, sauces on the side).
- Bundles (sandwich + fries + drink, pizza + sides for families) lift average order value.
- Typical spend: individual meals 35–55 AED, pizza meals 45–70 AED, family orders 120–200 AED.

## Cloud kitchens
- Dubai has many shared cloud kitchen operators; a cloud kitchen lets the brand test a catchment with lower capex than a dine-in unit.
- Tradeoff: low setup cost and speed vs. no walk-in brand visibility.

## Own channel
- Build a direct ordering channel (website or WhatsApp ordering) over time to reduce commission dependence, using loyalty offers to move repeat customers off aggregators.
