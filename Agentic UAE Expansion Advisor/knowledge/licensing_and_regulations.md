# Licensing and Regulations

General guidance only; always confirm current requirements and fees with the relevant authority or a licensed business setup consultant.

## Trade license
- Restaurants operating on the mainland need a commercial trade license from the emirate's economic department (Dubai: Department of Economy and Tourism; Abu Dhabi: Department of Economic Development).
- Free zone licenses are generally not suitable for a restaurant serving the local market directly.
- A local service agent or partner structure may apply depending on activity and ownership rules; foreign ownership rules have been relaxed for many activities, so confirm the current position.

## Food safety approvals
- Dubai: Dubai Municipality Food Safety Department approves kitchen layout, equipment and food handling, and inspects before opening.
- Abu Dhabi: Abu Dhabi Agriculture and Food Safety Authority (ADAFSA) regulates food establishments.
- Staff handling food usually need food safety training certificates and occupational health cards.
- Kitchen design plans are typically reviewed before fit-out, so engage early to avoid costly rework.

## Halal and import requirements
- All meat must be halal certified; imported meat and ingredients need compliant labelling and certification.
- Pork and pork-derived ingredients are heavily restricted; keep the menu fully halal to avoid complexity.

## Other approvals
- Civil defence approval for fire safety in the fit-out.
- Signage and outdoor seating permits are separate from the main license.
- Alcohol licensing is not relevant to this concept.

## Tax
- UAE VAT is 5%; menu prices shown to consumers must include VAT.
- Corporate tax applies to business profits above the threshold; plan for registration and bookkeeping from day one.

## Typical timeline
- Licensing, approvals and fit-out usually take several months; budget a buffer between lease signing and opening, and negotiate a rent-free fit-out period.
